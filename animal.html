MEET THE PREDATOR

Born to Rule

The jaguar is the largest cat in the Americas and one of the strongest predators in the world. Its powerful body, beautiful spotted coat and incredible hunting abilities make it truly unique.

Jaguars are excellent swimmers and usually live in tropical forests, wetlands and grasslands.

WHERE THEY LIVE

THE WILD HOME

Jaguars are found mainly in Central and South America. The Amazon rainforest is one of their most important natural habitats.
